Why Stretch Therapy

Stretching on your own can be difficult to keep up with. Long hours of sitting, driving, standing or exercise can leave the body feeling tight. Some stretches are also hard to do safely without help.

🪑 Desk work, driving and long days on your feet quietly tighten the hips, shoulders and lower back.
⏳ A solo stretch routine is easy to start and even easier to let slide after a busy week.
🤲 Many of the most effective stretches need someone else to apply gentle, controlled pressure.

That's where Nurtured comes in.

You can relax while I guide you through each stretch. The session is adjusted to your comfort and the areas that need the most attention.

The Experience

After your session: Feel lighter, looser and more relaxed.

With regular sessions: Improve flexibility, mobility and everyday movement.

With professional support: Stretch safely and make good use of your session time.

🏠
The best part? I come to you.

I bring the session to your home, office or another private location, so you do not need to travel.

Services

What I Offer

Simple treatments to help with stiffness, muscle tension and everyday movement.

🤸

Stretch Therapy

Guided stretching for tight muscles and limited movement. This suits people who feel stiff after sitting, driving, travelling, exercising or doing housework.

Session length: 60 minutes
👐

Soft Tissue Manipulation

Manual treatment for tight muscles and stubborn knots. It is often useful for the neck, shoulders, back and legs.

Session length: 60 minutes
⭕

Cupping

Cups create gentle suction on selected areas of the body. Many people use cupping for muscle tension in the back, shoulders or legs. Temporary round marks can appear and usually fade.

Session length: 30 minutes
